Privacy policy

Leafy Bee Studio · effective 29 October 2026

Leafy Bee Studio is a private application used only by the owners of the Leafy Bee channels (Gabriel and Bella) to publish their own videos to their own YouTube channel, Instagram account and Facebook Page. This policy explains what it accesses, what it keeps and how to remove its access.

YouTube API Services

Leafy Bee Studio uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What it accesses

  • YouTube (only the Leafy_Bee channel): uploading videos; setting their titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails, captions, privacy status and publish time; reading back the channel's own identity and the status of its own videos to confirm each upload.
  • Instagram (only @leafy_bee_inc) and Facebook (only the Leafy Bee Inc Page): publishing the owners' Reels and posts, and reading back the account identity and the status of those posts.

It does not read comments, messages, followers, subscribers, viewers or any other person's data, and it does not access any account other than these three.

What it stores, and where

The sign-in tokens that Google and Meta issue are stored only on the owners' own Windows computer, in Windows Credential Manager. Records of what was published (video IDs, links, times and status) are kept in the owners' local project files. Nothing is stored on any server run by Leafy Bee Studio; there is none.

What it shares

Nothing. Leafy Bee Studio does not sell, rent, share or transfer any data to anyone. It sends the owners' own videos and their descriptions only to the platform they are being published on. It uses no analytics, advertising or tracking. Its use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing its access and deleting data

  • Google / YouTube: remove "Leafy Bee Studio" at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Facebook / Instagram: remove it under Facebook Settings → Business integrations (and Instagram Settings → Apps and websites).

Removing access stops it at once. The owners delete the stored tokens from Windows Credential Manager and the local records whenever they choose; to ask about anything stored, write to the contact below.
